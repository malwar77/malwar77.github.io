This separation means you can test each piece alone, replace one piece without breaking the others, and explain the program to someone else by walking through its parts in order. The example below shows that style in miniature, using only the Python standard library and files on your own machine.

When you think about from structure to working code, start with the person who will use the tool rather than with the cleverest method you know. A real tool earns its place by solving one small problem reliably, day after day, without demanding attention or creating new work. In AIDay that means the feature must work on the reader's own notes, on their own machine, with plain files they can open and read. If a feature needs a network connection, a secret key, or someone else's computer, it does not belong in this project. Keeping that test in mind makes every later choice simpler, because you can reject ideas quickly and spend your energy on the parts that genuinely help.
