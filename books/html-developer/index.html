What this book is about

This book takes you from your first HTML page to building real websites you can actually use. You will learn elements and attributes, headings and text, links and images, lists and tables, forms, semantic structure, media, accessibility, validation, responsive images, templates and structured meaning — then move into performance, security, publishing and working with CSS and JavaScript.

Along the way you will work with real examples you can open in a browser, understand how a web page is put together and how browsers, search engines and assistive technology read it, and finish by planning, building and polishing a complete site of your own. By the end, you will be able to write clear, accessible HTML, build multi-page websites, and finish a project you can show to others.
