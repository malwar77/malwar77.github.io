Front-End Developer — Full Teaching Book

Learn front-end development from the ground up: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, testing, performance, security, and a capstone project — the MyDay Dashboard. Every chapter has 50 sections, each ending with a check question, plus a practical task and chapter questions at the end.

Book format (used for every book in this series)

  • 50 chapters per book.
  • 50 sections per chapter.
  • Every section teaches one idea in plain words, with a short example where one helps.
  • Every section ends with a check question.
  • Every chapter ends with a practical task you build yourself, plus chapter questions.
  • No copied textbook text: all lessons are original explanations.
  • Safety rule for the security and AI books: only test systems you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorised access is a crime, including under Nigeria's Cybercrimes Act. AI safety sections teach how safeguards work and how to test your own systems defensively — they do not provide ready-to-use prompts to bypass live systems you do not control.
