Section 50: What You Can Do Next with Blocks and Procs

This section is about what you can do next with blocks and procs. In plain words, What you can do next with blocks and procs is to use them in arrays, hashes, and Enumerable methods, turning stored behaviour into everyday tools. That idea may sound simple, and it is simple on purpose. Ruby was designed to read a little like ordinary language, so when you understand the idea in words first, the code that follows feels familiar instead of strange. For example, a program might store a filter proc and a transform proc, then apply both to a list of data.

Here is how it works in practice. The next chapter, on arrays, gives you plenty of data to practise on, and procs give you the behaviour to practise with. When you are unsure what a program will do, slow down and trace it by hand, pretending that you are Ruby and following each line yourself. This habit of tracing is one of the most useful skills you will ever build, and it works for every topic in this book, not just this one.
