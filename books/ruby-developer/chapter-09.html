In plain words, What you can do next with arguments and blocks is to learn procs and lambdas, which let you store blocks in variables and pass them around. That idea may sound simple, and it is simple on purpose. Ruby was designed to read a little like ordinary language, so when you understand the idea in words first, the code that follows feels familiar instead of strange.
