What it does

  • Mock location: tap the map or search for a place to teleport, simulate routes with waypoints and loop, in-app joystick, favourites and history.
  • No ads, no time limits: mock location is free and ungated — no rewarded videos, no banner, no countdown.
  • WireGuard VPN client: import your own .conf file for a genuine WireGuard tunnel, with live status and a kill-switch option.
  • Local VPN (DNS filter): on-device DNS filtering that blocks known ad and tracker domains. It filters DNS only and does not change your IP address.

The local VPN section is honestly labelled in the app as DNS-only.

Setup after installing

  1. Open the downloaded APK to install it. If Samsung or Chrome warns that the app is blocked as dangerous, that warning appears because the app is sideloaded from outside the Play Store: tap More details, then Install without inspection.
  2. Turn on Developer options, then go to Developer options → Select mock location app and pick kelGPS (not any other similarly named app).
  3. Open the app, tap the map or search for a place, then Teleport here or Start spoofing.
  4. To stop Android killing the service mid-route, set the app’s battery usage to Unrestricted.
Use it responsibly. Some apps and games detect mock locations and may restrict or ban accounts that use them. Do not use this app to cheat in games or to misrepresent your location to a service that forbids it.
